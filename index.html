I've recently become familiar with the concept of a web "shrine." Just a place on a website, perhaps a page or section or even the whole thing, that is dedicated to a subject of one's passion. We are sort of doing this in class, but I want this site I make to serve as an opportunity to infodump about one of my most intense interests besides just writing or art: creepypasta, and the history that comes with it. I want it to host factual information, historical context as well as my own opinions and analyses of different creepypasta, while evoking different eras (perhaps simultaneously?) of spooky internet nostalgia through its presentation. I have attempted this once before with my first coded website, though it was much more personal in aesthetic and I didn't do very much with it. Disorienting tiled gifs, glittery dividers, kind of ugly fonts, using every square inch of the page--I want to coin the term "old internet gothic". Maybe someone has come up with that before me but that's what I want it to feel like. I'd like to be able to embed videos as well, possibly audio as well as animation to make the site feel alive (or like it WAS alive long ago). Old decrepit web and the concept of the web "lifespan" is something very fascinating to me and the subject of creepypasta lends itself super well to that (one of my primary examples is itself a 25 year old website that could be considered both "dead" and "alive").
